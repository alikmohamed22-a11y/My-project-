Mount Rushmore was never finished. The presidents were supposed to have bodies.
In 1927, sculptor Gutzon Borglum started carving four presidents into a South Dakota mountain.
His plan: carve them down to the waist. Add a giant history inscription. And behind the heads, a Hall of Records.
Workers dangled over the cliff in bosun chairs. About 90% of the rock came off with dynamite.
Jefferson started on Washington's right. The rock was cracked, so they blasted him off and moved him to the left.
So why do we only get heads?
You see, the money kept running out. Then, in March 1941, Borglum died.
His son Lincoln wrapped up the faces, and that October, the work stopped. No bodies. No inscription. Just a 70-foot tunnel to nowhere.
And in 14 years of blasting, not one worker died.
Here's the twist. In 1998, history panels were finally sealed in a vault at that tunnel's entrance.
But the presidents? They're still waiting on their bodies.
