# Example 2: "Acoustic Kitty" (the CIA spy cat, 1960s)

- **Timeline:** `story_kitty.js` has 14 shots over 1,674 frames (56 s). Each shot names a light preset and a set: `room:<lab|office|home>`, `dc` or `void`.
- **Sets:**
  - `rooms.js`: 1960s CIA lab, CIA office (with the memo, stamp and cash pile) and a living room, at x = −800.
  - `dc.js`: a 1960s Washington street and park, embassy with red flag, taxi, surveillance van and sedans, at z ≈ 800.
- **Cat:** `cat.js` is the original Oddverra tabby. Poses: sit, walk, sleep, lick, stare, hungry, startled, yawn. Gear can be switched on, and there is a blueprint mode.
- **Cast:** the 1960s outfits are in `main.js` under ODD: AGENT1/2, SOV1/2, SURG1/2, GRANNY. The `fedora` and `surgcap` hats are in `oddverra_next.js`.
- **Sound:** `audio_kitty.py` holds the 60s spy-jazz bed (walking bass, brushes, vibes, horn hits), which cuts out at the taxi and comes back softly for the payoff. It also has synth SFX: meow, purr, tyre screech, typewriter and beeps.
- **Facts:** the taxi death is told as "the famous version" because it is disputed. Victor Marchetti's account says the cat was hit by a taxi; Robert Wallace's says it wasn't. The 1967 memo "Views on Trained Cats" concluded the idea would not be practical.
