Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing... than to the pyramids.
Sounds wrong? Let's do the math.
The Great Pyramid of Giza was finished around 2560 BC. Pharaoh Khufu wanted a tomb, and he wanted it big. Thousands of workers. Giant ramps. Very heavy rocks.
Now, fast-forward. Every marker on this road is a century. Keep going. Keep going...
Cleopatra is born in 69 BC. That's about 2,500 years after the pyramid.
To her, the pyramids weren't new. They were ancient tourist attractions. Greek travelers like Herodotus had visited them four centuries before she was born.
And Cleopatra wasn't even Egyptian by blood. Her family was Macedonian Greek. She was reportedly the first in her dynasty to actually learn Egyptian.
Now fast-forward again. All the way to 1969. Apollo 11 lands on the Moon.
That's only about 2,000 years.
2,500 years back. 2,000 forward.
Cleopatra is closer to the astronauts... than to the pyramid builders.
Which means the pyramids were older to Cleopatra... than Cleopatra is to you.
