The most famous water gun ever made was invented by a NASA spacecraft engineer... by accident.
Lonnie Johnson was a nuclear engineer who worked for the Air Force, and for NASA's Jet Propulsion Lab.
He helped power the Galileo probe to Jupiter, and later worked on Cassini, bound for Saturn.
But in 1982, at home, he was building a heat pump that used plain water instead of Freon.
He hooked a test nozzle to his bathroom sink... and a jet of water blasted right across the room, into the tub.
His first thought? This would make an amazing water gun.
So he built one, out of PVC pipe, Plexiglas, and an empty soda bottle.
Toy companies weren't so sure. It took him years to find a maker.
You see, in 1990, a company called Larami launched it as the Power Drencher. A year later, it got a new name: the Super Soaker.
It reportedly sold $200 million worth in 1991 alone, and became one of the best-selling toys of all time.
Years later, he took Hasbro to arbitration over unpaid royalties. In 2013, he won nearly $73 million.
The man who helped send a probe to Jupiter... is best known for soaking your little brother.
