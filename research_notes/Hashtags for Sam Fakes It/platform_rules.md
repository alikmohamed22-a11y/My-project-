# Platform Hashtag & Keyword Rules: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-09. US market focus. Primary/official sources are marked [OFFICIAL]. Vendor blogs (Later, Hootsuite, schedulers, SEO tools) have an incentive to oversell "hacks" and are marked [VENDOR]. Many vendor blogs repeat each other without citing primary sources.

## Instagram: hashtag limit, Mosseri/@creators guidance, Reels recommendations, search/SEO, followability

### Takeaway
Instagram cut the hashtag cap from 30 to **5 per post/Reel**, announced by @creators on Dec 18, 2025 with a gradual rollout. A few early-2026 blogs said the Help Center still showed 30 and the rollout was uneven, so check the current cap in-app. Mosseri has said repeatedly (Feb and May 2025) that hashtags **do not increase reach**. They mainly label what a post is about. Discovery now depends on recommendations plus keyword search over captions, names/bios and alt text, and Google can index professional accounts' posts. Hashtags are no longer followable (since Dec 2024).

### Cited Findings
- **The 5-hashtag cap (Dec 2025):** @creators posted on Threads that "Instagram will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five." The post added: "We find that using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones… can improve both your content's performance and people's experience on Instagram." Social Media Today published this on Dec 18, 2025. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- The previous cap was 30. Instagram had earlier tested a 3-hashtag cap for some users. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/); [Business Today, Dec 19 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)
- The cap applies to both posts and Reels. Coverage framed it as an anti-"hashtag stuffing" measure and said Instagram called generic tags such as #explore and #reels less effective than targeted ones. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)
- Instagram's stated reasons were more relevant hashtag use and less misuse by spammers and scammers. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075)
- **Conflicting reports on status in 2026:** Circleboom [VENDOR] said Instagram's help page still listed up to 30, and Storrito [VENDOR] said in late Jan 2026 that users were seeing the limit in-app warnings but there was "no official announcement." The second claim conflicts with the @creators post above. Most 2026 guides treat 5 as the rule. — [Circleboom](https://circleboom.com/blog/how-many-hashtags-should-you-use-on-instagram-in-2026/); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/instagram-hashtag-limit-30-to-3-what-users-are-seeing/); [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/instagram-limits-hashtags-5-organic-reach-strategy-2026)
- Some vendor guides say hashtags in comments count toward the same per-post total. This is unverified from an official source. — [Fanpage Karma via search summary](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/how-many-hashtags-should-you-use-on-instagram/); [RecurPost](https://recurpost.com/instagram-scheduler/instagram-hashtag-limits-and-placement-rules/)
- **Mosseri on reach**, as summarized by Social Media Today:
  - Feb 2025: hashtags "don't work" to increase reach.
  - May 2025: hashtags don't improve visibility but are "a great way to let people know what a post is about and connect posts."
  - Social Media Today also notes that AI recommendation systems now drive more content discovery.

  — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- Business Today said Mosseri "has recently reiterated that hashtags are no longer the primary driver of reach," and that Instagram's systems now prioritize caption keywords, on-screen text and the visual content itself. It gave no direct quote. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)
- A vendor blog says Mosseri restated in a 2026 AMA that hashtags are not designed to increase reach. I could not verify this against a primary source. — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-hashtags-reach/)
- **Following hashtags was removed in December 2024.** Most reports give Dec 13, 2024 as the date. One outlet gave Dec 31. Hashtags can still be added, searched and tapped to browse posts. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-removing-option-follow-hashtags/); [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1832229/instagram-discontinued-following-hashtags-explained); [Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-is-getting-rid-of-the-ability-to-follow-hashtags)
- **The "Recent" tab on hashtag pages:**
  - Instagram removed it in the US in 2020 around the election.
  - In April 2022 it tested pages showing only "Top" and "Reels" tabs.
  - I found no reliable 2024–26 confirmation of the tab's current status.

  — [TechCrunch, Apr 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/19/instagram-removes-recent-tab-hashtag-pages/amp/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-tests-removing-the-recent-tab-from-hashtag-searches/622344/)
- **Search/SEO:** Instagram search matches typed keywords against usernames, names, bios, captions, hashtags and places. Vendors also cite alt text. — [Sociality.io](https://sociality.io/blog/instagram-search-features/) [VENDOR]; [Later](https://later.com/blog/cheat-instagram-algorithm/) [VENDOR]
- **Google indexing:** Starting July 10, 2025, public posts from professional (business/creator) accounts can appear in Google Search. This is on by default and can be switched off. — [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-posts-are-now-showing-up-in-google-search) [VENDOR]
- Vendor data: one analysis of 1.6M posts found a "measurable but limited" effect from hashtags. — [Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/?p=13492) [VENDOR]

### Inferences
- For a brand-new account, Instagram hashtags are mainly a **topic/classification label**, not a reach lever. Use 3–5 specific tags (niche, series or brand) rather than generic ones like #reels or #fyp, which Instagram itself calls less effective.
- Keyword-rich first lines of captions, on-screen text, a descriptive name field/bio and alt text are more useful for search and for Google indexing, since Google indexes captions and not hashtag pages.
- A branded series hashtag is still useful as a browsable archive for viewers who tap it, but there is no follow-hashtag feed to rely on.

### Gaps
- No direct access to help.instagram.com hashtag pages (fetch returned empty), so I could not confirm the Help Center's current wording on 5 vs 30.
- No primary Mosseri video or post transcript was retrieved; quotes come via Social Media Today.
- Whether hashtags in comments count toward the cap is not officially confirmed.
- Status of the "Recent" tab on hashtag pages in 2026 is unknown.
- I found no official Instagram statement that on-screen text or spoken audio is used for search ranking. Business Today asserts it without a quote.

## TikTok: caption/hashtag limits, official guidance, search SEO, recommended hashtag count

### Takeaway
TikTok has **no official cap on hashtag count**. Hashtags count toward the caption limit, which is widely reported as **4,000 characters in-app** (2,200 via some API/scheduler routes). TikTok's own guidance says hashtags "can support discovery, but more doesn't always mean better," and that relevant, accurate language describing the video matters most. TikTok says it uses captions, sounds and hashtags as video information for recommendations. Search-SEO advice to put keywords in caption, on-screen text and speech is near-universal, but the specific mechanics come from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok's Creator Academy article "Getting discovered with Search" contains the "more doesn't always mean better" line and the advice to use relevant, accurate language. I saw this in a search snippet; the full page did not render on fetch. — [TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/Getting-discovered-with-Search) [OFFICIAL]
- TikTok's Discover is a place to explore trending videos, hashtags, creators and sponsored content, so hashtags remain browsable/searchable. — [TikTok Support: Discover and search](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/discover-and-search) [OFFICIAL]
- TikTok has said it uses video information such as captions, sounds and hashtags to recommend videos. This is long-standing guidance from TikTok's 2020 "How TikTok recommends videos" post, cited here via a third-party summary. — [TikTok discover page summary](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/should-i-add-hashtags-to-my-tiktok-video)
- **Caption limit:** the usual figure is 4,000 characters in-app. Several schedulers report 2,200 when posting through the Content Posting API. AdaptlyPost [VENDOR] gives a different breakdown: 2,200 for video captions and a separate 4,000 description field for photo posts. — [RecurPost](https://recurpost.com/tiktok-scheduler/tiktok-character-limits-and-hashtags/); [Replug](https://replug.io/blog/tiktok-character-limits); [AdaptlyPost](https://adaptlypost.com/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit) [VENDOR]
- **Hashtag count:** vendor claims conflict. One says only the first 5 hashtags count, another says "up to 30." Both agree there is no official TikTok limit. I found no official confirmation of a 5-hashtag cap on TikTok. — [RecurPost](https://recurpost.com/tiktok-scheduler/tiktok-character-limits-and-hashtags/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/tiktok-limits-complete-guide) [VENDOR]
- Common creator/vendor recommendation: 3–5 relevant hashtags. — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/tiktok-caption-keywords-discoverability); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-seo/) [VENDOR]
- **Search SEO, vendor consensus:** keywords should appear naturally across the caption, on-screen text and spoken audio. Vendors say TikTok reads on-screen text (OCR) and speech, and advise keywords in the first ~3 seconds. — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/tiktok-caption-keywords-search-seo); [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/?p=175466); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-seo/) [VENDOR]
- Unverified vendor claims:
  - Keywords in the first ~100 caption characters get more weight.
  - Native in-app text is read better than burned-in text.
  - TikTok can detect and penalize unnatural keyword repetition.

  — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/tiktok-caption-keywords-discoverability); [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/tiktok-seo-how-to-get-more-views-with-tiktok-keywords/) [VENDOR]
- **For You feed (FYF) eligibility:** TikTok's 2025 Community Guidelines revision covers content "misleading about matters of public importance or harmful to individuals." It dropped the long itemized list of FYF-ineligible content. — [TechCrunch, Aug 15 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/15/tiktoks-new-guidelines-add-subtle-changes-for-live-creators-ai-content-and-more/)
- TikTok can make an entire account ineligible for recommendations after repeated violations, starting with a warning strike. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/tiktok-is-trying-to-clean-up-its-for-you-recommendations-210057825.html)

### Inferences
- For a new TikTok account, hashtags are a weak secondary signal. Use 3–5 accurate ones: topic, niche and series/brand. Avoid #fyp-style filler, which official guidance implicitly discourages ("more doesn't always mean better").
- The higher-leverage move is saying the topic keyword out loud, putting it in on-screen text, and putting it early in the caption, so TikTok search and the classifier can match it.
- Misleading hashtags, such as trending tags unrelated to the video, risk falling under the misleading-content and spam rules.

### Gaps
- The full Creator Academy article text could not be fetched (JavaScript-rendered), so wording is from a search snippet.
- I found no official TikTok document confirming OCR or speech-to-text weighting in search ranking, or any hashtag-count cap.
- I found no TikTok newsroom 2025–26 post specifically on hashtags.

## YouTube Shorts: how hashtags work, #shorts status, official guidance

### Takeaway
YouTube's official help page says:
- Hashtags can go in titles and descriptions.
- **Up to 3 of the "most engaging" description hashtags** show above the title. YouTube picks them; they are not necessarily the first three.
- **More than 60 hashtags means all of them are ignored.**
- Over-tagging and misleading or unrelated tags can lead to removal from uploads or search.

YouTube does not say #shorts is required. Shorts are classified by format (vertical, short length), not the tag. In a July 2026 YouTube tips piece, YouTube itself said tags have lost reach value. A single relevant hashtag is fine.

### Cited Findings
- Hashtags can be added to the title and description of videos and Shorts. Up to three of the most engaging description hashtags show above the title, and all remain in the description. Clicking one opens a results page. Typing # in a Short shows suggested hashtags. — [YouTube Help: Hashtag policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658) [OFFICIAL]
- "If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, each hashtag on that content is ignored." Over-tagging "can lead to removal" from uploads or search. Hashtags must relate directly to the video, or they may be removed. Ordinary descriptive tags or repeated sentences in the description (the "non-hashtags" rule) may lead to removal or penalties. Hashtags cannot contain spaces. Harassing, hateful, sexual or vulgar hashtags are prohibited. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658) [OFFICIAL]
- Playlist titles and descriptions also support hashtags, which is relevant for series. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658) [OFFICIAL]
- In YouTube's own Shorts tips, Culture & Entertainment Editor Julia Lee Harter said tags have lost reach value, but relevant hashtags reveal trends and formats and a hashtag's page is "a gold mine of ideas." — [Social Media Today, Jul 19 2026](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-offers-tips-on-working-with-shorts-videos/825096)
- **#shorts:** I found no official statement that it is required. A vendor says YouTube has identified Shorts primarily by vertical aspect ratio and length since 2024. Another vendor still calls #Shorts "required," which conflicts. — [PostLinkApp](https://postlinkapp.com/blog/youtube-shorts-hashtags) vs [MetadataReactor](https://metadatareactor.com/blog/youtube-shorts-seo-guide/) [VENDOR]
- One vendor claims more than 15 hashtags causes all to be ignored. This is not supported by the official page, which says 60. — [search summary of third-party guides](https://www.lilachbullock.com/best-hashtags-youtube-shorts/) [VENDOR, unverified]
- YouTube tested suggested hashtags for Shorts in 2024. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-experiments-with-suggested-hashtags-for-shorts-clips/639748/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/381291/youtube-tests-suggested-hashtags-for-shorts-creato.html)

### Inferences
- One to three relevant hashtags, such as a series/brand tag plus one topic tag, is fully within policy and is the practical sweet spot. A single short hashtag is fine.
- Adding #shorts is harmless but likely not necessary for classification.
- The title text (keywords) matters more than hashtags for YouTube search.
- A consistent series hashtag plus a playlist gives viewers a browsable series page.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube statement on whether hashtags influence Shorts feed recommendations, beyond the editor's "lost reach value" remark.
- The official #shorts status is not documented on the retrieved pages.

## Official stance: hashtag stuffing, banned/spammy tags, series / "Part 1" labeling

### Takeaway
All three platforms officially discourage stuffing:
- Instagram capped hashtags at 5 explicitly to curb spam.
- YouTube ignores all tags when there are more than 60, and can remove content for over-tagging or misleading tags.
- TikTok says "more doesn't always mean better," and its misleading-content and spam rules can make content or accounts ineligible for the For You feed.

I found **no official guidance from any platform on "Part 1"/series labeling**.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram's 5-tag cap exists to "reduce misuse by spammers and scammers." Instagram says targeted tags beat generic ones. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- Hashtag follows were removed in Dec 2024, a move reporters tied to hashtags being easy to game and spam. — [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1832229/instagram-discontinued-following-hashtags-explained)
- YouTube's over-60 rule, its over-tagging/misleading-tag removal rules and its non-hashtag spam rule are covered in the YouTube section above. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658) [OFFICIAL]
- TikTok: repeat violations can make a whole account ineligible for recommendations. Misleading content may be made FYF-ineligible during fact-checking. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/tiktok-is-trying-to-clean-up-its-for-you-recommendations-210057825.html); [TikTok Transparency: misinformation](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/combating-misinformation/) [OFFICIAL]
- TikTok has a reporting flow for hashtags, which implies hashtag-level moderation. — [TikTok Support: Report a hashtag](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/report-a-problem/report-a-hashtag) [OFFICIAL]
- Vendor suggestion for series: use one consistent branded/series hashtag across every video in a series. — [YouTube hashtag guide summary](https://insights.vaizle.com/best-hashtags-for-youtube-shorts/) [VENDOR]

### Inferences
- A unique branded series tag (e.g. a show-name hashtag) is low-risk on all three platforms and doubles as a browsable index.
- Pairing it with 1–3 accurate topic tags stays within every platform's guidance.
- "Part 1/Part 2" labeling is a creative choice with no official rules either way. YouTube playlists and the series features on Instagram/TikTok are the official structures, though I did not research those in depth here.

### Gaps
- I found no official Instagram, TikTok or YouTube guidance on "Part 1"/series labeling in captions.
- I did not find Instagram's current list of banned/restricted hashtags. Instagram historically hides or restricts posts under abused tags, but there is no 2025–26 source for this.
- TikTok's and Instagram's 2025–26 official series features (TikTok Series, Instagram linked Reels) were not researched.
