# Hashtags for "Sam Fakes It" (2D animated comedy series — Day 1 of 30, fake chef) on IG Reels / TikTok / YT Shorts, 2025–2026

Method note: Instagram counts below were pulled on 2026-11-09 by fetching best-hashtags.com tag pages (each page shows "uses in last 7 days", "posts per hour", and lifetime IG post counts for related tags; page "last update" dates range from Oct 2024 to Nov 2026 and are noted where relevant) and displaypurposes.com. Third-party trackers disagree, sometimes by 50x, so treat all counts as order-of-magnitude. I could not get reliable TikTok per-tag post/view counts (TikTok Creative Center is not scrapeable without login, and no search result reported them). That gap is listed below.

Size buckets used: HUGE = 10M+ IG posts (or 50K+ uses/week); MID = ~500K–10M; SMALL/NICHE = under ~500K.

## Q1. Candidate hashtags by theme, with size/competition (Instagram counts; TikTok where known)

### Takeaway
The animation tags that are big and active are #animation (~16M IG posts, ~51K uses/week) and #cartoon (~21–26M, ~67K/week). The best mid and niche tags for a new account are #2danimation (~1.6K uses/week), #animator (~893K posts), #animationmeme (~159K), #animatedseries (~80K), #comedyskit (~190K), #chefslife (~2.4–2.8M), #restaurantlife (~700K), #storytime (~2.4–3.5M) and #webseries (~5.9K uses/week). Very specific tags (#cookingfail, #kitchenfails, #animatedstory, #storytimeanimation, #animatedcomedy, #chefhumor, #jobhumor) showed about 0 posts per hour on the tracker. They are fine for categorization but bring almost no browse traffic.

### Inferences
- **Recommended Instagram mix (max 5, see Q3):** 1 anchor animation tag (#animation or #2danimation), 1 format tag (#animatedseries or #storytime), 1 topic tag (#restaurantlife or #chefslife), 1 comedy tag (#comedyskit or #relatable), and 1 own series tag (e.g. #SamFakesIt). Of these, #2danimation, #animatedseries, #comedyskit and #restaurantlife are the mid/niche sweet spot: they are active but not flooded. #chefslife is food-photo heavy, so it reaches professional cooks who may enjoy the satire.
- **TikTok (no hard cap documented in sources):** add TikTok-native tags such as #animationtiktok, #chefsoftiktok, #kitchentok/#restauranttiktok (the last two are my suggestions and unverified) and #storytime, plus the series tag and "#day1"-style episode tags. TikTok search also indexes caption keywords.
- **YouTube Shorts:** use 3 tags in the description (#animation #storytime #SamFakesIt or #cookingfail); these show above the title.
- **Low-value tags:** the exact-match fail tags (#cookingfail, #kitchenfails) and #animatedstory are cheap to include as categorization on TikTok and YT, but they will not bring browse traffic. On IG's 5-tag budget they are lower value than #restaurantlife or #comedyskit.
- **Huge tags:** #funny, #memes, #comedy, #cooking and #chef move hundreds to thousands of posts per hour, so a 0-follower post is buried within minutes. They also attract a real-food audience that may not want animation.
- **Series tag:** a branded series tag (#SamFakesIt) mirrors Weird Helga's #weirdhelga practice and gives a binge path through episodes, which matters for "Day X of 30".

## Q2. Do generic "algo" tags (#fyp, #foryou, #viral, #explore, #reels) help?

### Takeaway
There is no official confirmation that they help. On Instagram, the head of the platform says hashtags in general don't significantly increase reach. On TikTok, one 2026 observational study found that videos tagged #fyp did about 17% better than their account's median, but it is correlational: creators may add #fyp to videos they already expect to do well. Under IG's 5-tag cap, spending slots on #explore, #reels or #viral is wasteful. On TikTok, one #fyp is harmless and optional.

### Cited Findings
- Statista ranks #fyp as the most-viewed TikTok hashtag (as of Oct 2025). View counts include any video carrying the tag, so this is not evidence of a boost. — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1299851/tiktok-top-hashtags-by-post-views)
- The SocialCrawl study (data pulled 2026-08-10) covered 40 accounts and 1,569 videos, using views normalized to each account's own median:
  - Any hashtags vs none: 1.012 vs 0.975 (~3.7% lift).
  - 7+ hashtags: ~9.5% lift, but five heavy-tagger accounts supply 46% of that bucket.
  - 4–6 hashtags: essentially no lift.
  - #fyp present: 1.153 vs 0.988 (~16.7%).
  - The fyp-family (#fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage, #viral): ~21.5%.
  - The authors call it correlational and non-random. Mega accounts (mkbhd, nasdaily) use no hashtags.
  — [SocialCrawl](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/do-hashtags-work-on-tiktok)
- TikTok has not confirmed that #fyp affects distribution, and experts say it isn't a "magic ticket". — [StackInfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/what-does-fyp-mean/); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/tiktok-hashtags-go-viral/)
- #fyp has ~54.2M IG posts and #viral ~143.7M. Both are HUGE and meaningless as IG categories. — [best-hashtags (related-tag tables)](https://best-hashtags.com/hashtag/funny/)
- Adam Mosseri (early 2025): "Hashtags do not significantly increase your reach"; hashtags are a categorization tool. Some outlets note that Instagram's own in-app tips still say relevant hashtags "can help you reach more people". — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-updates/instagram/hashtags-dont-affect-reach/); [Flick](https://www.flick.social/learn/blog/post/instagram-hashtags-mosseri); [Digital Diplomacy](https://wearedigitaldiplomacy.substack.com/p/hashtags-still-relevant-adam-mosseri?open=false)
- On Instagram, keywords in the caption and on-screen text are described as a stronger discovery signal than hashtags. — [Phable](https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery)
- A Skillshare animation instructor says generic #FYP gets "drowned out" and recommends specific tags. — [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/animate-with-procreate-design-and-animate-your-tiktok-persona/1793686045?via=custom-list)

### Inferences
- **TikTok:** 3–6 relevant tags plus at most one #fyp is a reasonable, low-cost hedge. Relevance (topic tags plus caption keywords like "animated", "chef", "cooking fail", "storytime") matters more for search and interest matching.
- **Instagram:** skip #explore, #reels, #viral and #fyp entirely. With a 5-tag cap, each slot should go to a topical tag.
- **YouTube Shorts:** #shorts is commonly added, but I found no evidence it is required in 2025–26. Unverified.

### Gaps
- No controlled (causal) test of #fyp was found.
- No official YouTube source was found on whether #shorts still matters.

## Q3. Platform rules and limits that shape the tag list (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Instagram capped hashtags at 5 per post/Reel in December 2025, after testing 3. YouTube shows the first 3 description hashtags above the title, and guides say that more than ~15 tags causes all of them to be ignored (unverified with YouTube). TikTok has no comparable cap documented in these sources, though guides advise ≤10–12.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram limits posts and Reels to 5 hashtags, announced via @creators around 2025-12-18 and replacing the 30-tag limit. Earlier December reports described a 3-tag test. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-tests-three-hashtag-limit-leaving-small-creators-worried-85146.htm)
- One guide says the 5-tag limit counts the caption and first comment together. — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/how-many-hashtags-instagram/)
- On YouTube Shorts, the first 3 description hashtags appear as clickable links above the title. Hashtags placed in the title suppress that row. Guides say more than 15 hashtags causes YouTube to ignore all of them (no official citation found). The usual recommendation is 3–5 tags. — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/YouTube-hashtags/); [HashtagTools](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/youtube-shorts-hashtags-title-vs-description-2026); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/best-hashtags-youtube-shorts/)
- On TikTok, a guide advises no more than 10–12 hashtags. — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/tiktok-storytelling-strategy)

### Inferences
- Build one master list, then cut per platform: IG gets 5, YT gets 3 (plus up to ~5 more lower in the description), TikTok gets ~4–7.

### Gaps
- I did not reach the official Instagram Help Center text or YouTube Help text directly. The 15-tag YouTube rule is unconfirmed.

## Q4. Banned / restricted tags to avoid in this niche

### Takeaway
Instagram publishes no official banned list; community lists are unreliable and change. Of the tags you might reach for, the risky ones are engagement bait (#followforfollow, #tag4like, #tagsforlikes, #mustfollow) and a few innocuous-sounding words that third-party lists flag (#adulting, #single, #humpday, #hustler, #killingit, #hardworkpaysoff, #workflow, #desk, #eggplant, #pornfood). None of the core animation, chef or storytime tags were flagged.

### Cited Findings
- Ayrshare's compiled list (originally published 2024-02-12, updated 2026-07-15) includes:
  - Engagement bait: #followforfollow, #tag4like, #tagsforlikes, #mustfollow.
  - Comedy/relatable-adjacent: #humpday, #adulting, #single, #singlelife, #alone, #always, #thought, #todayimwearing.
  - Work-adjacent: #hustler, #killingit, #hardworkpaysoff, #workflow, #desk, #kickoff.
  - Food: #eggplant, #pornfood.
  - No animation-specific tags.
  The list is compiled from third parties. One use usually affects only that post; repeated use risks a shadowban. — [Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/blog/avoid-using-these-instagram-banned-hashtags/)
- To check a tag on Instagram, search it: no page or zero posts means banned; a limited page or guideline notice means restricted. IQHashtags claims 115K+ banned tags as of Aug 2026; this is a vendor figure. — [IQHashtags](https://iqhashtags.com/banned-hashtags-list); [Bundle.social](https://bundle.social/blog/banned-hashtags-instagram)

### Inferences
- Avoid #adulting and #worklife-style "relatable" tags flagged on these lists. Use #relatable or #comedyskit instead.
- Avoid fandom tags (#bobsburgers, #adultswim) unless the content actually references them.
- The theme also suggests a further caution (my inference, not from the lists): avoid fire, weapon or "kitchen accident" style tags. Knife or fire gags are fine visually, but tags implying real injury could trip sensitive-content filters.

### Gaps
- No TikTok or YouTube banned-tag lists specific to this niche were found. TikTok does not publish one.
