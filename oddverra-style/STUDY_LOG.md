Patterns across every video:
- Very few hard cuts. The camera moves between scenes instead.
- Something changes on screen every 1–2 words.
- Every line is shown literally.
- Packed worlds.
- Lighting follows the mood.
- Big emotions on every face.
- There's a doubter.
- A one-line twist ending.
